HouseParty Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

HouseParty ("we", "our", "the app") helps people discover and host small events and house parties. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled. If anything here is unclear, contact us at nippunsingla07@gmail.com.

Who this app is for

HouseParty is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We verify date of birth at signup and do not knowingly allow accounts for anyone under 18.

Information we collect

  • Account & profile: email address, full name, username, profile photo, short bio, and date of birth.
  • Identity verification: a photo of a government-issued ID (front, and back for non-passport documents) so a human reviewer can confirm you're a real, of-age person before you can host or join events. These images are stored in a private location that only you and our review team can access — they are never public and never shown to other users.
  • Location: your device's location, used to show nearby events on the map and to let hosts set an event's location. Other users only see an event's general city/area; the exact address is revealed only to the host and to guests whose request has been accepted.
  • Event & booking content: events you create (title, description, photos, schedule, price, capacity), booking requests you send or receive (including any message you add), and reviews you give or receive about other users.
  • Photos: images you choose to upload for your profile or an event, accessed via your device's camera or photo library with your permission.
  • Safety reports & blocks: details you submit when reporting a user or event, and the list of users you've chosen to block.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier used to deliver notifications about your bookings, if and when push notifications are enabled for your device.
  • Basic technical data: standard operational data (such as device type and crash diagnostics) collected by our hosting and app-distribution providers to keep the app running securely. We do not currently use a separate analytics or advertising tracking service.

How we use this information

  • To operate the core features of the app: showing nearby events, letting you create and manage events, and handling booking requests between hosts and guests.
  • To verify your identity and age before granting hosting/booking privileges, for the safety of everyone using the app.
  • To let hosts and guests review each other after an event, building a trust signal for the community.
  • To review and act on safety reports.
  • To send you notifications about your bookings (accepted/declined requests, new requests to your events), where enabled.
  • To troubleshoot and keep the service secure and reliable.

What other users can see

Your name, username, profile photo, verification badge, and public host/guest ratings are visible to other users, as is any event you publish. Your date of birth, phone number (if ever added), exact home address, and ID documents are never shown to other users.

Who we share information with

  • Other users — only what's described above as publicly visible.
  • Service providers who host and run the app on our behalf, strictly to provide their service: our database/authentication/file-storage provider, our maps and location-search provider, and our app-distribution/push-notification provider. They do not have independent rights to use your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal & safety — we may disclose information if required by law, or if necessary to investigate a safety report or protect the rights and safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your controls

  • You can edit your profile, and unblock any user you've blocked, at any time from within the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, events, booking history, and reviews. ID verification images are deleted as part of this process, except where we're required to retain records for a limited period for legal or safety compliance.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, associated data is removed as described above.

Children's privacy

HouseParty is not directed at, and may not be used by, anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your data can be sent to nippunsingla07@gmail.com.
